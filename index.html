NULLTRACK

A mobile-native audio tracker for iOS.

About

Nulltrack is a phone-native audio tracker: a step-sequenced music workstation built from the ground up for touch screens. Synth, sampler, modulation, mixer, effects, and AUv3 plugin hosting — no external keyboard required.
