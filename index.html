00 About

Nulltrack is a phone-native audio tracker: a step-sequenced music workstation built from the ground up for touch screens. Synth, sampler, modulation, mixer, effects, and AUv3 plugin hosting — no external keyboard required.

Nulltrack for iOS is coming to the Apple App Store. Updates will be posted here as the release approaches.

02 Inside

00SYNSynth engines — dual-oscillator subtractive synth and a dedicated bass synth engine, 12-voice polyphony per track
01SMPSampler — load your own samples or the factory library, per-track sample playback
02MODModulation — LFOs, AHD / ADSR / drum envelopes, velocity, random, key tracking; up to 17 destinations per instrument
03SEQSequencer — per-step probability, conditions, transpose, retrigger; polymetric phrase lengths
04FXCFX commands — three FX columns per step, 52 tracker commands: arps, vibrato, retrigs, stutter, nudge, cut…
05MIXMixer — per-track strips, 8 global send buses, 4 insert slots per track, master metering + limiter
06AUVAUv3 hosting — run third-party Audio Unit instruments and effects inside your song, state saved with the project
